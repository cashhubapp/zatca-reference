# Changelog

- 2026-10-26: Fines on every page from the May 2024 guide. Our e-invoicing fines page and two guides quoted ZATCA's November 2021 figures. All of them, and the penalty calculator, now use the classification guide (May 2024): a warning first, then fines from the second offence. `data/penalties.json` added.
- 2026-10-26: E-invoicing reference launched. We published the first stage of the reference: the glossary, the integration waves table and the limits, methodology and corrections pages. Codes, tools, the comparison and the guides follow in stages, each logged here.
- 2026-07-24: ZATCA announces Wave 25. Wave 25 covers VAT-registered businesses whose taxable revenue passed SAR 187,500 in 2022, 2023, 2024 or 2025. The deadline to integrate with Fatoora is 1 February 2027.
- 2026-06-29: Fines-cancellation initiative extended to 31 December 2026. ZATCA extended the initiative by six months, from 1 July 2026 to 31 December 2026. It covers late registration, late payment and late filing fines, and VAT return correction fines. The announcement does not list e-invoicing offences, so do not assume they are covered.
- 2025-09-26: ZATCA announces Wave 24. Wave 24 covers businesses whose revenue passed SAR 375,000 in 2022, 2023 or 2024, with an integration deadline of 30 June 2026.
- 2025-06-27: ZATCA announces Wave 23. Wave 23 covers businesses whose revenue passed SAR 750,000 in 2022, 2023 or 2024, with an integration deadline of 31 March 2026. The announcement is in Arabic; its English page did not open when we checked.
